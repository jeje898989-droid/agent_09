# Card News Plan: War by the Numbers (v1)

- **Topic:** War
- **Concept:** "The Earth is fighting more than at any time in 80 years": war in 2025, told through numbers
- **Format:** 8 cards (card 1 is the thumbnail), Instagram carousel
- **Data baseline:** 2025 figures, published April–June 2026 (SIPRI, UCDP, UNHCR, UN)
- **Language of the card copy:** Korean (the text inside each card block is the copy to put on the card)

## 1. Planning overview

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Core message | War is not only in history books. In 2025 it set new records. |
| Target | Social media users in their 20s and 30s who rarely follow international news |
| Goal | Show the big picture in one carousel, and get people to save and share it |
| Tone | Calm, factual and neutral. No graphic or shocking images. Let the numbers speak. |
| Structure | Hook (1) → Overview (2) → One big number per card (3–6) → People behind the numbers (7) → Wrap-up and CTA (8) |

## 2. Card-by-card plan

### Card 1: Thumbnail

- **Role:** Stop the scroll and make people swipe.
- **Card copy:**
  - Main title: `지금 지구는 80년 만에 가장 많이 싸우고 있다`
  - Subtitle: `숫자로 보는 2025년의 전쟁`
  - Small label (top): `카드뉴스 | 세계`
  - Swipe cue (bottom right): `넘겨보기 →`
- **Visual:** Dark navy background with a world map in thin lines. Red-orange dots mark conflict areas. Put the title in large, bold type in the center, and set "80년" in the accent color.

### Card 2: 2025 at a glance

- **Role:** Preview the four key numbers.
- **Card copy:**
  - Heading: `2025년, 세계가 남긴 4개의 숫자`
  - `65` 무력 분쟁
  - `8` 국가 간 전쟁
  - `2조 8,900억 달러` 세계 군사비
  - `70명 중 1명` 집을 잃은 사람
- **Visual:** A 2 × 2 grid with one big number and a short label in each cell. Each number then gets its own card (cards 3–6), in the same order.

### Card 3: 65 armed conflicts

- **Card copy:**
  - Big number: `65건`
  - Heading: `2025년 무력 분쟁 수, 2차 세계대전 이후 최다`
  - Body: `이 중 13건은 한 해 전투 사망자가 1,000명을 넘는 '전쟁' 수준이었습니다.`
  - Source: `출처: 웁살라 분쟁 데이터 프로그램(UCDP), 2026`
- **Visual:** A simple line chart that ends at a peak (conflict count over time), with the 2025 point in the accent color. Keep the axes minimal.

### Card 4: 8 wars between states

- **Card copy:**
  - Big number: `8건`
  - Heading: `나라와 나라가 싸운 전쟁, 1년 새 2배`
  - Body 1: `1946년 집계 시작 이후 가장 많은 수치입니다.`
  - Body 2: `러시아–우크라이나, 이스라엘–이란, 인도–파키스탄 등`
  - Body 3: `러시아–우크라이나 전쟁이 국가 간 전쟁 전투 사망자의 62%를 차지했습니다.`
  - Source: `출처: UCDP, 2026`
- **Visual:** Two bars side by side, "2024: 4" and "2025: 8". Add a small donut chart for the 62%.

### Card 5: $2.89 trillion in military spending

- **Card copy:**
  - Big number: `2조 8,900억 달러`
  - Heading: `세계 군사비, 11년 연속 사상 최대`
  - Body 1: `세계 GDP의 2.5% — 2009년 이후 가장 높은 비중`
  - Body 2: `미국·중국·러시아 3개국이 전체의 51%`
  - Source: `출처: 스톡홀름국제평화연구소(SIPRI), 2026`
- **Visual:** A stack of coins or a bar that rises step by step over 11 years. Show the top-3 share as a pie split.
- **Note:** Do not convert the figure to Korean won on the card, because the exchange rate changes. If you do, write the date of the rate.

### Card 6: 1 in 70 people forced from home

- **Card copy:**
  - Big number: `70명 중 1명`
  - Heading: `전쟁·박해로 집을 떠난 사람 1억 1,780만 명`
  - Body 1: `대한민국 인구의 2배가 넘는 규모입니다.`
  - Body 2: `10년 만에 처음 줄었지만, 여전히 역대 최고 수준입니다.`
  - Source: `출처: 유엔난민기구(UNHCR) 글로벌 동향 보고서, 2026`
- **Visual:** A grid of 70 person icons with one of them highlighted in the accent color.

### Card 7: The people behind the numbers

- **Role:** Turn the statistics into people.
- **Card copy:**
  - Heading: `숫자 뒤에는 사람이 있습니다`
  - Body 1: `2025년 전쟁으로 피해를 입은 것이 확인된 아이들: 24,174명 (30년 만에 최다)`
  - Body 2: `세계 아이들 5명 중 1명이 분쟁 지역에 살고 있습니다.`
  - Body 3: `실제 피해는 확인된 숫자보다 더 클 것으로 보입니다.`
  - Source: `출처: 유엔 아동과 무력분쟁 연례보고서(2026), PRIO`
- **Visual:** Warm but muted tones, such as a silhouette of a child's hand or a backpack. Do not use photos of injured people.

### Card 8: Wrap-up and CTA

- **Card copy:**
  - Heading: `기억해야 할 숫자`
  - Summary: `65 · 8 · 2조 8,900억 · 70명 중 1명`
  - Message: `관심은 평화의 첫걸음입니다. 숫자를 기억해 주세요.`
  - CTA: `저장하고, 함께 알리고 싶은 사람에게 공유해 주세요.`
  - Sources (small text): `SIPRI(2026), UCDP(2026), UNHCR(2026), UN 아동과 무력분쟁 보고서(2026)`
- **Visual:** Return to the dark navy background of card 1 so the carousel feels complete. Put the four numbers in one row.

## 3. Common design guide

| Item | Guide |
|---|---|
| Size | 1080 × 1350 px (4:5, Instagram portrait) |
| Colors | Background: dark navy `#14213D`; text: white `#FFFFFF`; accent for numbers: red-orange `#E63946`; secondary: gray `#A8A8A8` |
| Fonts | Korean: Pretendard or Noto Sans KR. Big numbers: ExtraBold, 120–180 pt; headings: Bold, 48–60 pt; body: Regular, 28–34 pt |
| Layout | Big number at the top, one-line heading, then 1–3 short body lines. Keep a 60 px margin from every edge. |
| Text amount | At most 3 body lines per card. Keep each line short enough to read in two seconds. |
| Sources | Put the source at the bottom of every data card in small gray text. |
| Images | Icons, maps and simple charts. No graphic war photos. |

## 5. Fact-check notes

- All figures are 2025 data published in 2026. Before posting, check the original reports (SIPRI, UCDP, UNHCR, UN) for any updates.
- The UCDP figures (65 conflicts, 8 interstate wars, 62%) come from news coverage of the UCDP data. Confirm them against the primary UCDP publication.
- The 117.8 million figure is UNHCR's number for the end of 2025. Some articles show 117.3 million, so use the UNHCR figure.
- The child figures are verified UN cases, so the real numbers are likely higher. Card 7 already says so.
