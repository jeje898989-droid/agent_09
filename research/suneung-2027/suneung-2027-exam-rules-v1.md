# Research: 2027 CSAT (수능) exam-room rules and accidental cheating (v1)

- **Date:** 2026-10-31
- **Purpose:** Fact base for a Korean Naver blog post for test-takers and parents: what changed in exam-room rules this year, and mistakes that accidentally count as cheating (부정행위)
- **Method:** Web search, then a check of each cited page with a direct fetch

**Evidence labels used below**

- **[P]** Primary source, content read directly (a KICE or ministry page or image, or a ministry release reposted by a public body).
- **[P-partial]** Primary file that could only be partly decoded. See "Gaps and caveats".
- **[S]** Not checked against a primary source. Press or other secondary page only.
- **[V]** Vendor or commercial page. Not checked against a primary source.

## Summary

- **Dates:** The exam is on Thursday 2026-11-19 and scores are notified on 2026-12-11 [P]. The 예비소집 date of 2026-11-18 is confirmed only by press and a vendor page [S].
- **New this year:** KICE's own materials say smart glasses are banned and that the 가채점표 cannot be filled in after the end bell [P]. That it is printed on the back of the 수험표, and that it is new this year, comes from press reports [S].
- **Applicants:** The official total is 551,864, down 2,310 from 554,174. Graduates (졸업생) are 173,706, up 13,784 [P-partial, with labels matched to press]. The earlier conflict is resolved: 554,174 is last year's total.
- **Most common cheating cases:** In the 2022-2026 academic years there were 1,229 cases. Writing after the end bell led with 479 (39.0%), then failing to hand in banned items with 348 (28.3%), and Period 4 method violations with 286 (23.3%) [S, ministry data given to a lawmaker].
- **Penalty:** KICE says a cheating finding voids the whole exam, not just that period [P]. Press reporting KICE adds that wearing a smart device during the exam also suspends eligibility for the next year's exam [S].

## What is new this year

**Official 가채점표 on the back of the 수험표**

- Press on the detailed plan: "수험표 뒷면에는 가채점표가 인쇄돼 수험생이 시험 시간 중 가채점표를 작성할 수 있다" and "종료령 이후 가채점표를 작성하는 행위는 부정행위로 처리된다" [S] [10], dated 2026-06-29. YTN also reports the printing and describes it as new this year [S] [11], dated 2026-06-30.
- The 노컷뉴스 report is confirmed as you cited it: "시험 종료령이 울린 후 가채점표에 답안을 작성하는 행위도 부정행위로 처리되므로 주의해야 한다". It also says not to attach a separate 가채점표 to the 수험표 [S] [1], dated 2026-10-19.
- KICE's own leaflet states: "종료령이 울리면 가채점표도 작성 불가" [P] [4], dated 2026-10-15.
- KICE's Period 4 leaflet lists continuing to write or correct answers for a finished subject after its end bell, with "(가채점표 포함)", as a type of cheating [P] [7], dated 2026-10-16.
- Vendor page: only the form printed on the back of the 수험표 may be used for self-scoring [V] [8].

**Smart glasses ban**

- The 노컷뉴스 report is confirmed as you cited it: smart glasses are banned because of concern over AI-enabled devices, and proctor guidance gains identification tips from October [S] [9], dated 2026-06-30.
- KICE leaflet, on all electronics: "휴대 또는 가방에 보관만 해도 부정행위에 해당 / ※ 스마트안경도 포함" [P] [4].
- KICE's banned-items sheet lists 스마트안경 among communication devices [P] [12], dated 2026-10-16.
- Press reporting KICE's guidance [S], all dated 2026-10-15 or 10-16:
  - Keeping smart glasses in a bag falls under 부정행위 9호 and voids the exam [13].
  - Wearing them during the exam without handing them in first falls under 3호, which voids the exam and suspends eligibility for one year [13].
  - The rule applies whether or not the device is used [14], [15].
  - Students must take off their glasses and put them on the desk when a proctor asks. Refusal can be reviewed and treated as equivalent to 3호 [13], or as serious cheating [14], [15].

**Pre-existing rules, not new.** Press from 2025 shows that the analog-watch rule, handing in devices before Period 1, and the Korean History requirement already applied last year [S] [16]. The blog post should not present these as changes.

## Mistakes that count as cheating

**Banned items, per KICE sheet [P] [12]**

- Heading: "시험 시간, 쉬는 시간 불문하고 적발 시 부정행위 처리".
- "시험 중 활용 여부 및 기능과 무관하게 모든 전자기기는 원칙적으로 시험장 반입이 금지됨".
- Communication (including Bluetooth) or payment functions: phones, smartwatches, smart glasses, tablets, earphones, and so on.
- Electronic displays (LCD, LED, etc.): electronic dictionaries, tumblers with electronic displays, watches, and so on.
- Any other rechargeable item: e-cigarettes, power banks, and so on.
- If a banned item is brought in unavoidably, it must be handed to the proctor before Period 1 starts. "1교시 시작 전에 제출하지 않은 경우 모두 부정행위로 간주".

**Items allowed during the exam, per KICE sheet [P] [12]**

- 신분증, 수험표, black computer-use 사인펜, white correction tape, black pencil, eraser, black 0.5 mm 샤프심, and a mask (checked in advance by the proctor).
- **Watch:** An analog watch with hour and minute hands (a second hand is also allowed), with no payment or communication function (including Bluetooth) and no electronic display.
- Writing tools other than black pencil, black computer-use 사인펜 and 샤프 cannot be carried personally.
- Items of unclear status are checked by the proctor each period, and medical or physical-need items (magnifier, earplugs, cushion, etc.) need a check before each period.

**Items outside the permitted list [P] [12]**

- They may be held during breaks, but must go into a bag and be put at the front of the room or in the hallway before the exam starts.
- Keeping them during the exam, or storing them somewhere other than as the proctor directed, is treated as cheating even if they are not carried on the person.
- Confiscated if found: transparent paper (oil paper), practice pads, **personal 샤프**, plus pens for pre-marking, ballpoint pens, and so on.
- Treated as cheating if found: textbooks, reference books, past exam papers, and so on.

**샤프 and writing tools**

- "샤프와 사인펜은 시험실에서 일괄배부 예정" [P] [4].
- "답안지에는 모두 검은색 컴퓨터용 사인펜만 사용"; the 필적 확인 문구 must also be written with it [P] [4].
- "샤프 사용, 수정테이프 떨어짐, 이중표기 등에 따른 불이익은 수험생이 감수해야 함" [P] [4]. This means marking the answer sheet with 샤프 is framed as the student's disadvantage, not as cheating. A personal 샤프 is listed as an item to be confiscated [P] [12].
- YTN says students may use their own 사인펜 [S] [11], and KICE's list allows it [P] [12].

**End bell**

- At the bell: put down writing tools at once, place the answer sheet on the right and the question paper on the left, and lower hands below the desk [P] [4].
- KICE warns that writing after the end bell has been caught through reports from other students in the room, and that unnecessary movements after the bell have been mistaken for writing and reported [P] [7].

**Period 4 rules [P] [4], [7]**

- Korean History is mandatory. "미응시하면 수능성적 전체 미발급".
- The 15 minutes between Korean History and Inquiry are for collecting and handing out materials, not a break.
- **Two inquiry subjects:** Keep only the first-subject booklet out. Fold the rest in half and put them with the cover in the personal storage envelope on the floor. For the second-subject period, take out only that booklet.
- **Cheating examples listed by KICE:**
  - Correcting or marking the first subject's answers during the second subject's time.
  - Swapping the answer sheet in the second subject's time to correct the first subject's answers or mark other answers.
  - Looking at the second subject's paper during the first subject's time.
  - Looking at both chosen subjects' papers at the same time.
- **One inquiry subject:** During the first-subject period, keep only the answer sheet, face down on the desk, and wait quietly. In the second-subject period, take out only the chosen booklet and put the rest in the envelope.
- A paper other than your chosen subjects left on the desk is collected, and cannot be swapped for your own paper in the envelope.

**Also worth warning about**

- Mark the 문형 on the answer sheet for Periods 1, 2, 3 and Korean History ("홀짝 문형표기 주의") [P] [4]. This is an answer-sheet error risk, not a cheating rule.

**Penalties**

- KICE: "부정 행위로 처리될 경우 해당 교시 뿐만 아니라 당해 시험 모두 무효 처리됨" [P] [7].
- Voiding plus a one-year suspension for next year's exam is reported for wearing a device during the exam, or for 3호 cases [S] [13], [14], [15].
- Press, citing Higher Education Act Article 34(5)-(7) through a regional education office: the exam is voided, and intentional or serious cheating brings a one-year suspension [S] [20], dated 2024-11-15. This is the article's summary, not statute text.
- Simply possessing a banned device (9호) is reported to void the exam only [S] [13].

## Practical items for 예비소집 and exam day

- **예비소집 (2026-11-18):** Students collect the 수험표 and receive rules instruction [S] [1].
- **Exam-day documents:** "수험표, 신분증 (모바일 신분증 불가)" [P] [4]. Vendor page lists accepted IDs: resident card, driver's license, valid passport, youth card, qualifying student ID [V] [8].
- **Reissue of a lost 수험표:** Bring one photo printed from the same negative as the application photo, and report to the exam-site management office by 08:00 on exam day [V] [8]. Press on last year's guidance gives similar steps, and says a temporary card is available without a photo [S] [16], for the 2026 academic year. No primary confirmation for this year.
- **Entry:** Into the assigned room by 08:10 [P] [4].
- **Before leaving home:** Check bags and pockets for phones, power banks, e-cigarettes, and electronic watches. This is advice drawn from the banned-items list [P] [12] and the case counts [S] [14].

**Not confirmed against a primary source**

- 예비소집 on 2026-11-18, and whether attendance is required this year. Last year's press said it was required [S] [16]. Times and places are set locally.
- The 가채점표 being printed on the back of the 수험표, and being new this year. KICE materials I read confirm the end-bell rule but do not show the printing.
- The 3호 and 9호 labels, and the one-year suspension.
- 수험표 reissue steps.
- The Period 5 time, the 16:07 start of the second inquiry subject, and subject labels for the timetable.
- The past-case counts. The original ministry data was not found.

**Conflicts and oddities between sources**

- **Detailed plan date:** KICE's site says 2026-07-01 [2], Herald says it was announced on 06-30 [10], and the basic plan said 07-06 [24], [3]. I treat the KICE date as correct.
- **Exam year in one article:** My fetch of [9] rendered the exam date as "2027년 11월 19일". KICE gives 2026 [2]. This may be an article or extraction error.
- **"Three-year ban":** A related headline on [15] mentions criminal referral and a three-year ban for AI glasses. I could not confirm this for the CSAT. The three-year ban in [13] applies to a university essay exam at University of Seoul, so it should not be used for the CSAT.
- **Possession penalty:** A device kept in a bag is voiding only per [13], while [14] gives the voiding and one-year limit in the context of wearing. Press is consistent on this split, but the rule text was not read.
- **샤프:** [8] says personal 샤프 cannot be used; KICE's sheet agrees and lists it as confiscated [12]. A mechanical pencil is not cheating in itself on this evidence, so the post should not say so.

**Not used**

- Older counts for 2020-2024 (1,174 cases) appear in search results, but those pages were not opened.
- A regional tally for the 2026 exam day also appeared only in search results.
- Blog-style guides were not used as sources.
